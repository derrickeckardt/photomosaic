"""
BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS:

Fixed Costs (Monthly):
  Hosting (Heroku/AWS):           $500
  Database/Storage:               $200
  SSL/Domain:                     $15
  Monitoring/Analytics:           $100
  Stripe processing fees:         $200 (built into margins)
  Salaries (part-time initially): $2,000
  Misc/Emergency:                 $500
  
  Total Fixed: $3,515/month

Variable Costs (Per Order):
  Printful fulfillment:           ~60% of selling price
  Payment processing (Stripe):    2.9% + $0.30
  Customer service:               ~$2 per order
  
  Average total variable: ~65% of order value

BREAK-EVEN POINT:
  Average order: $40
  Gross profit per order: $14 (35%)
  Monthly fixed costs: $3,515
  
  Orders needed per month: 3,515 ÷ 14 = ~251 orders
  
  At 5% conversion from users:
  Need 5,000 active monthly users
  
  CONSERVATIVE ESTIMATE:
  Break-even by month 4-6 of operation

RUNWAY CALCULATION:
  Initial investment needed: $10,000
  Monthly burn rate (early): $3,515 - $2,000 from orders = $1,515
  Runway: 10,000 ÷ 1,515 = 6.6 months
  
  With month 1: $500 orders → break-even by month 6
  With month 2: $1,000 orders → accelerate to break-even
  With month 3: $2,000 orders → cash flow positive
"""

# RISK MITIGATION

"""
KEY RISKS & MITIGATION:

Risk 1: Printful dependency
  - Mitigation: Integrate with 2-3 print partners
  - Mitigation: Build direct customer relationships
  - Mitigation: Keep margins reasonable (not exploitative)

Risk 2: Image copyright issues
  - Mitigation: Clear terms of service
  - Mitigation: DMCA reporting mechanism
  - Mitigation: User attestation they own rights
  - Mitigation: IP insurance

Risk 3: Customer acquisition too expensive
  - Mitigation: Focus on organic/viral growth
  - Mitigation: Strong referral program
  - Mitigation: Community building
  - Mitigation: Influencer partnerships

Risk 4: Tech fails (image processing)
  - Mitigation: Comprehensive error handling
  - Mitigation: Quality assurance testing
  - Mitigation: Multiple server infrastructure
  - Mitigation: Customer support for failures

Risk 5: Market saturation
  - Mitigation: Build brand loyalty early
  - Mitigation: Niche focus (mosaics specifically)
  - Mitigation: Product differentiation
  - Mitigation: Community/social features

Risk 6: Payment fraud
  - Mitigation: Stripe handles most fraud
  - Mitigation: Implement fraud detection
  - Mitigation: Monitor chargebacks
  - Mitigation: Insurance for fraud losses

Risk 7: Scale/uptime issues
  - Mitigation: Use CDN (CloudFront)
  - Mitigation: Multiple servers/load balancing
  - Mitigation: Database replication
  - Mitigation: Monitoring/alerting
  - Mitigation: Disaster recovery plan
"""
